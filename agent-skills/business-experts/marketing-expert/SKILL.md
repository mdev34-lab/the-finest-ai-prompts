## Core Workflows

### Workflow 1: Integrated Marketing Campaign
1. **Audit & Research**: Analyze current marketing performance across all channels. Identify gaps, opportunities, and quick wins.
2. **Strategy Development**: Define campaign objectives, target audience, channel mix, messaging architecture, and success metrics aligned to business goals.
3. **Content Creation**: Develop platform-native content assets from shared strategic themes. Create variations for each channel's format and culture.
4. **Launch & Distribution**: Coordinate multi-channel publishing across owned, earned, and paid media. Optimize timing per platform.
5. **Monitor & Optimize**: Track performance in real-time. Adjust creative, targeting, and budget allocation based on data.
6. **Report & Iterate**: Measure campaign ROI. Document learnings. Feed insights into next campaign cycle.

### Workflow 2: SEO & Organic Growth Engine
1. **Technical Foundation Audit**: Crawl site (crawlability, indexation, Core Web Vitals, structured data). Fix critical issues first.
2. **Keyword Universe & Topic Clusters**: Build comprehensive keyword portfolio grouped by search intent. Design pillar page + supporting content architecture.
3. **Cannibalization Audit (BLOCKER)**: Cross-page query map from Search Console. Resolve conflicts before any content changes.
4. **Content Creation & Optimization**: Create new content targeting identified gaps. Optimize existing pages with improved targeting, structure, and depth.
5. **Link Authority Building**: Execute digital PR, content-led link acquisition, and strategic outreach campaigns.
6. **AI Visibility Layer**: Implement AEO foundations (robots.txt AI crawler rules, llms.txt, token budgets). Optimize for AI citation (FAQ schema, entity signals, comparison content). Declare WebMCP actions for agentic task completion.
7. **Measurement & Iteration**: Weekly ranking tracking, traffic analysis by intent type, ROI reporting. Adjust strategy based on algorithm updates and performance data.

### Workflow 3: China Market Entry
1. **Signal Collection**: Aggregate hotlist data from 7+ China platforms. Log ranking, trajectory, lifespan, and cross-platform spillover events.
2. **Deep Analysis & Opportunity Extraction**: Apply four mental models (Signal Detection, Triangulation, Counter-Intuitive, MECE). Run Content Track + Comment Track analysis.
3. **Strategy Design & Localization**: Map opportunities to platforms based on audience-platform fit. Design platform-native content strategies with specific hooks, scripts, and visual guidelines.
4. **GTM Execution**: Break strategy into phased gates (P0-P5) with clear go/no-go criteria. Build executable checklists optimized for small teams.
5. **Measurement & Iteration**: Track against success metrics. Collect new comment and engagement data. Update opportunity matrix monthly. Retire expired signals, promote emerging ones.

### Workflow 4: Email Lifecycle Architecture
1. **Audit**: Map current state — lists, attributes, active sequences, complaint/bounce rates, DNS authentication records.
2. **Architect**: Design segment tree, attribute schema, and lifecycle state machine. Define which contacts get which content at which stage.
3. **Build**: Create sequences with timing, branching, exit conditions, and A/B variants. Map CRM events to ESP triggers. Configure authentication.
4. **Test**: Send test emails across clients. Verify dynamic content rendering. Check unsubscribe flow. Validate attribute mapping end-to-end.
5. **Launch**: Deploy to small segment first (10-20% of target). Monitor complaint rate hourly for first 24h.
6. **Optimize**: After 7-14 days, evaluate A/B results. Adjust send times, subject lines, content. After 30 days, assess sequence-level conversion rate.

### Workflow 5: Crisis Response
1. **Assess & Hold** (<30 min): Gather facts. Assess severity (Level 1-4). Identify affected stakeholders. Issue holding statement. Convene crisis team. Establish single spokesperson.
2. **Respond & Control** (<2 hours): Draft full response with Legal review. Brief internal stakeholders. Prepare FAQ for customer-facing teams. Monitor media and social in real time.
3. **Manage & Recover** (ongoing): Update on committed cadence. Document every media inquiry and response. Track sentiment shift. Identify recovery narrative.
4. **Post-Crisis Review**: What triggered it, what worked, what didn't. Update crisis playbook.

### Workflow 6: Podcast Launch & Growth
1. **Show Concept & Positioning**: Define target listener persona. Conduct competitive audit (top 20 shows). Identify unique angle. Create show bible.
2. **Content Engine**: Design flagship episode format. Build episode brief system. Establish topic sourcing pipeline (evergreen + trending + listener-sourced). Implement guest tier strategy.
3. **Distribution & Discoverability**: Optimize show and episode metadata for platform search. Produce clip strategy (3-5 shareable moments per episode). Set up newsletter integration and cross-promotion partnerships.
4. **Community & Monetization**: Launch listener community hub (Discord/Circle/WeChat). Develop sponsorship media kit. Activate listener support (Patreon/memberships). Design product ladder from passive listener to high-ticket client.

### Workflow 7: Campaign Architecture & Launch
1. Gather business objectives, target CPA/ROAS, available creative assets, and tracking infrastructure status
2. Pull account data via API or export — campaign structure, historical performance, search term reports, audience insights, conversion configurations
3. Design campaign architecture: tiered brand/non-brand/competitor/conquest structure, ad group taxonomy, naming conventions, label system
4. Select campaign types (Search, Shopping, PMax, Demand Gen, Social, Display) based on business objectives and audience readiness
5. Configure bidding strategy: evaluate conversion volume maturity, choose between tCPA/tROAS/Max Conversions/Max Conversion Value, set portfolio bid strategies
6. Build audience layers: first-party data activation, Customer Match, similar segments, in-market/affinity, exclusions
7. Write creative: RSA headlines/descriptions, social ad copy, display assets — aligned to platform specs and landing page message match
8. Deploy tracking: verify GTM container, GA4 events, conversion actions, enhanced conversions, server-side tags fire correctly
9. Set budget allocation across campaigns and platforms with pacing model
10. Launch and monitor learning period, impression share, frequency, and early conversion signals

### Workflow 8: Ongoing Optimization Cycle
1. Review search queries: pull search term reports, run n-gram analysis, identify waste, add negatives, surface new keyword opportunities
2. Audience refresh: check frequency, overlap, and fatigue metrics; refresh exclusions and expand lookalikes
3. Creative rotation: monitor ad strength, CTR trends, creative fatigue; launch new test variations bi-weekly per campaign
4. Bid and budget pacing: check budget utilization, learning period status, impression share changes; adjust bids and budgets
5. Tracking validation: cross-reference platform-reported conversions against GA4 and CRM; verify CAPI deduplication and enhanced conversion match rates
